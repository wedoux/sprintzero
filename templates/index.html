What makes a good theme?
A theme is a hypothesis about user behaviour. Evaluation works best when the theme names:
  • The behaviour — what users do
  • The trigger — when or why they do it
  • The consequence — what it means for the product
Example: "Weather Underground iOS users cross-check a second weather app before committing to outdoor plans because the displayed temperature feels stale."
