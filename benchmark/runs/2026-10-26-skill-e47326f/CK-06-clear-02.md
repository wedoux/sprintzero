### Falsification pass
- **Strongest counter-hypothesis:** The pause is an artefact of session structure, not participant behaviour — moderated sessions often have a natural lull around the five-minute mark (first task wrapping, moderator transitioning to the next prompt, participant waiting for instruction). The moderator may be noticing their own pacing pattern, not something about the participants.
- **What would overturn this finding:** Per-session timestamps of pause events across INT-01 through INT-06, showing either (a) pauses clustered at genuinely varied clock times unrelated to session structure, or (b) no such clustering at all. Session recordings reviewed by a second observer would also resolve it.
- **Where the evidence underdetermines the claim:** Almost everywhere. One moderator's cross-session impression cannot distinguish a participant behaviour from a session-structure artefact from a recall bias (five minutes is a round number the moderator may have reached for). The evidence base is a single sentence.
