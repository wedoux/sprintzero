**Evidence strength: STRONG**
As a descriptive claim about what one participant said in this sample, the finding is accurately and conservatively stated. It makes no generalization beyond the evidence.

One minor note: the claim says "rather than in-app browsing," but INT-02 only said what they *do* do, not what they *don't*. The contrast with in-app browsing is a light inference — defensible given "mostly," but a stricter wording would be "rather than another channel" or just drop the contrast.

### Falsification pass
- **Strongest counter-hypothesis:** INT-02 might also use in-app browsing significantly and simply named the friend-link channel as the most memorable or recent. "Mostly" in casual speech sometimes means "the thing that comes to mind," not a measured frequency.

- **Where the evidence underdetermines the claim:** The implicit contrast with in-app browsing is the only soft spot. The core descriptive claim is as well-supported as a single-quote claim can be.
