### Is it true for these people?

**Falsification pass**

### Tacit-knowledge prompt
INT-03 says "at this point" — did they say earlier in the session, or in a way the transcript doesn't capture (tone, a pause, a correction), whether this is a recent shift or how they've always used the app? That distinction changes what the quote means.

### Decision boundary
- **Supports:** That at least one long-streak user, in this sample, articulated streak-preservation as their main reason for opening the app, over a sense of learning.
- **Doesn't support:** Any claim about how common this is, whether it's a shift over time, whether other long-streak users feel the same, or anything about lapsed or new users (explicitly ruled out by the declared limit). It also doesn't support a decision to keep or drop the streak mechanic on its own.
- **What would close the gap:** A read across the other five sessions (INT-01, 02, 04, 05, 06) for the same pattern — do other long-streak participants describe their motivation similarly, or does INT-03 stand alone?

**Safe to act on:** Not yet — the claim is honest, but it's one quote from one person. It's a signal worth following up across the other five sessions before it informs the decision about the streak mechanic.
