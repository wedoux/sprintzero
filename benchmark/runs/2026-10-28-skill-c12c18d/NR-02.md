## Finding: "In this sample, one participant said they discover new podcasts mainly through a friend's link, rather than in-app browsing."

**Evidence strength:** WEAK
A single participant's remark in a five-session study. Real signal, but one voice.

**Claim matches its evidence:** Yes — the claim says "in this sample, one participant said", which is exactly what INT-02 shows.

### Is it true for these people?

**Falsification pass**
- **Strongest counter-hypothesis:** The participant may use in-app browsing routinely but recalled the friend-link moment because it was recent or memorable; "mainly" is their self-report, not observed behaviour.
- **What would overturn this finding:** A follow-up or diary entry from INT-02 showing substantial in-app browsing activity, which would contradict "mainly".
- **Where the evidence underdetermines the claim:** The word "mainly" rests on one self-report in one session; it is not corroborated by behavioural data or by other participants.

**Counter-evidence:** None in the material provided. The other four sessions (INT-01, INT-03, INT-04, INT-05) are not quoted, so whether they agree or disagree is unknown — this is a gap, not a confirmation.

**Unsupported claims:** None — the claim is restricted to what INT-02 said.

### Tacit-knowledge prompt (optional — ask only if a researcher who ran the sessions is available)
INT-02 said "mostly" — in the session, did they describe what in-app browsing they *do* do, or was the friend-link route the only discovery method they mentioned? That would help tell "mainly friends, some browsing" apart from "friends only".

### Decision boundary
- **Supports:** Noting, in a report, that at least one participant in this study described friend-sent links as their main discovery route.
- **Doesn't support:** Any claim about listeners in general, about the prevalence of friend-link discovery, or about whether the discovery tab is or isn't working — the sample is one person in a five-session study with no declared limits.
- **What would close the gap:** The equivalent quote or coded answer from the other four sessions on the same question (Q-01). That turns "one said" into "n of 5 said", which is the minimum needed to see whether this is a pattern or an outlier.

**Safe to act on:** Not yet — the claim is honestly stated, but it rests on one participant and the other four sessions in the study aren't reflected in the finding.
